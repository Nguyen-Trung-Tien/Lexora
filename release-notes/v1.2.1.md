# Lexora v1.2.1 — Pronunciation Lab & Automatic Updates
> Release Date: 2026-10-17 | Version: 1.2.1

## 🌟 Highlights
Lexora v1.2.1 introduces the dedicated **Pronunciation & Listening Lab**, background **Automated Software Updates**, and seamless runtime language switching between English and Vietnamese.

---

## 🚀 New Features
- **Pronunciation & Listening Lab**: Two-tier high-fidelity audio system featuring authentic human speaker recordings and Windows Speech Synthesis fallback with British (UK) and American (US) accent options.
- **Production Software Updates**: Automatically checks for new versions, downloads installers, displays rich release notes, and safely launches upgrades.
- **Dynamic Bilingual Support**: Switch effortlessly between English and Vietnamese in Settings without needing to restart the desktop app.

---

## ⚡ Improvements & UX
- **100% Offline-First Architecture**: All vocabulary terms, grammar rules, and cached pronunciation audio files remain stored locally on your PC.

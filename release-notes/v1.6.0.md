# Lexora v1.6.0 — Adaptive Roadmap & Target Ecosystem
> Release Date: 2026-11-05 | Version: 1.6.0

## 🌟 Highlights
Version 1.6.0 unveils the **Adaptive Learning Roadmap** module and introduces comprehensive system-wide keyboard shortcuts for effortless navigation across the entire desktop suite.

---

## 🚀 New Features
- **Adaptive Roadmap Engine**: Generates personalized exam preparation roadmaps tailored to your target score and available study timeframe for TOEIC, IELTS, and VSTEP.
- **Ecosystem Goal Synchronization**: Automatically links daily study targets with scheduled mock tests and milestone checkpoints.
- **Global Keyboard Shortcuts (Ctrl + 1 to Ctrl + 9)**: Instant keyboard switching between Dashboard, Vocabulary, Grammar, Review, Testing, and Roadmap workspaces.

---

## ⚡ Improvements & UX
- **Dynamic Ease Factor Calibration**: Automatically adjusts initial study difficulty for learners with larger proficiency score gaps to avoid early burnout.
- **Flawless Light & Dark Theme Adaptation**: Native Fluent 2 styling dynamically adapts to your Windows system preferences.

---

## 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Stability
- **Database Concurrency Protection**: Eliminated database collision risks when multiple pages access vocabulary records simultaneously.
- **Persistent Learning Progression**: Guarantees that active roadmaps and study progression are safely saved across application restarts.
